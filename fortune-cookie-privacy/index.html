Fortune Cookie Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how the Fortune Cookie app for Android (package name com.soluntion.fortunecookie), made by Soluntion Labs, handles information. Fortune Cookie does not ask you to create an account and does not send your personal information to us.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information on our own servers. Everything the app keeps (the fortunes you have cracked, your collection, your daily streak and your settings) is stored only on your device. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

ADVERTISING

Fortune Cookie is free and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob. This includes banner ads, occasional full-screen ads, and optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch for a bonus cookie. To show and measure ads, and to help prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected and handled by Google, not by Soluntion Labs. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalized ads, in your device's settings (on most devices: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Security & privacy > Ads).

YOUR CONSENT CHOICES

If you are in a region where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message the first time you open it, and you can choose whether to allow personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings screen.

NOTIFICATIONS

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device and contain no tracking. You can turn them off in the app's Settings screen or in your device's notification settings at any time.

SHARING A FORTUNE

When you choose to share a fortune, the app creates an image on your device and hands it to the app you pick, using Android's standard share feature. We do not receive a copy, and what happens next is controlled by the app you shared with.

WHAT WE DON'T DO

We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, location permission or any account login. We do not sell your information, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Fortune Cookie is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the app gains new features. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.

CONTACT US

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@soluntion.com.
