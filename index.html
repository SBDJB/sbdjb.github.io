Tracker Radar

Find hidden Bluetooth trackers

  • Scan for nearby AirTags, Samsung SmartTags, Tile and Google Find Hub trackers in seconds
  • Live proximity locator with audio pings that speed up as you get closer
  • Tells trackers near their own owner apart from ones that aren't, to cut false alarms
  • Premium: background alerts when a tracker follows you, full scan history and no ads

Privacy policy

Scan results are processed on your device and never uploaded. Available in 10 languages.

Pressura

Blood pressure log

  • Log systolic, diastolic and pulse in seconds, with American Heart Association categories
  • Scan your monitor's display with the camera; the photo is read on your phone and deleted
  • Day, week, month and year trends, plus a daily reminder
  • Export your readings to share with your doctor

Privacy policy

Pressura is a logbook, not a medical device, and does not give medical advice.
