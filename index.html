Tracker Radar

Find hidden Bluetooth trackers

  • Scan for nearby AirTags, Samsung SmartTags, Tile and Google Find Hub trackers in seconds
  • Live proximity locator with audio pings that speed up as you get closer
  • Tells trackers near their own owner apart from ones that aren't, to cut false alarms
  • Play a tracker's built-in sound to pinpoint it (AirTag and Find Hub devices)
  • Premium: background alerts when a tracker follows you, full scan history, automatic re-scanning, and no ads
