Pressura Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains how Pressura – Blood Pressure Log for Android (package name com.soluntion.pressura), made by Soluntion Labs, handles information. Pressura does not ask you to create an account and does not send your readings or other personal information to us.

YOUR BLOOD PRESSURE READINGS

The readings you log (systolic and diastolic pressure, pulse, the date and time, and any note you add) are stored only on your device. We do not collect, receive or store them on any server, we cannot see them, and they are never shared with advertisers. They are deleted when you delete a reading, clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Your readings leave your device only if you choose to export them. The "Export readings" option creates a CSV file on your device and hands it to the app you pick (for example email or a cloud drive) using your device's standard share feature. We do not receive a copy, and what happens next is controlled by the app you shared with.

SCANNING A READING WITH THE CAMERA

If you use the camera button, your device's camera app takes a photo of your blood pressure monitor's display. Pressura reads the numbers from the photo using text recognition that runs entirely on your device (Google ML Kit, with the model built into the app). The photo is not uploaded anywhere, and it is deleted as soon as the numbers have been read. Pressura itself does not request camera permission.

ADVERTISING

Pressura is free and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob, unless you buy "Remove ads" (see below). This includes a banner ad and occasional full-screen ads. To show and measure ads, and to help prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as your device's advertising ID, your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected and handled by Google, not by Soluntion Labs. Your blood pressure readings are never given to Google or any advertiser. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalized ads, in your device's settings (on most devices: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Security & privacy > Ads).

REMOVE ADS PURCHASE

You can make a one-time "Remove ads" purchase inside the app. After it, the app shows no ads and does not request them from Google AdMob. The purchase is handled entirely by Google Play: your payment details and purchase history are processed by Google under its own privacy policy and terms, and are never shared with us. The app only stores a note on your device that ads have been removed, and asks Google Play to confirm your purchase so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or use another device signed in to the same Google account.

YOUR CONSENT CHOICES

If you are in a region where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message the first time you open it, and you can choose whether to allow personalized ads. Where required, you can change your choice at any time from "Ad privacy choices" in the app's Settings screen.

NOTIFICATIONS

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. The reminder is scheduled on your device, is skipped on days you have already logged a reading, and contains no tracking. You can turn it off in the app's Settings screen or in your device's notification settings at any time.

WHAT WE DON'T DO

We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, location permission or any account login. We do not sell your information, we do not use your health information for advertising, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

NOT MEDICAL ADVICE

Pressura is a logbook for readings you take with your own blood pressure monitor. It is not a medical device, it does not measure blood pressure, and it does not give medical advice. The categories it shows follow American Heart Association adult guidelines for information only. Talk to your doctor about your readings.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Pressura is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the app gains new features. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
