Fortune Horoscope Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how the Fortune Horoscope app for Android (package name com.soluntion.fortunehoroscope) and for iPhone (bundle ID com.soluntion.fortunehoroscope.ios), made by Soluntion Labs, handles information. Fortune Horoscope does not ask you to create an account and does not send your personal information to us.

INFORMATION YOU ENTER

To personalise your horoscope, the app asks for your name, your date of birth and your gender (you can choose "Prefer not to say"). The app uses your date of birth to work out your zodiac sign, and uses these details together to put together your daily, monthly and yearly readings. These details are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else, and they are not used for advertising. You can change them at any time from the edit button on the main screen, and they are deleted when you clear the app's data or remove the app from your device.

INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We do not collect, store or receive any personal information on our own servers. Everything the app keeps (your profile, which readings you have revealed and your settings) is stored only on your device. We cannot see it.

ADVERTISING

Fortune Horoscope is free and is supported by ads provided by Google AdMob, unless you buy "Remove ads" (see below). This includes banner ads, occasional full-screen ads, and optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch to see a sneak peek of tomorrow's reading. To show and measure ads, and to help prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as your device's advertising identifier (Android's advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA on iPhone if you allow tracking), your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This information is collected and handled by Google, not by Soluntion Labs. Your name, date of birth and gender are never shared with Google. You can learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalized ads, in your device's settings (on most devices: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Security & privacy > Ads).

On iPhone, the app may ask whether you allow it to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Google cannot use your device's advertising identifier and you will see less personalized ads; the app works the same either way. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

REMOVE ADS PURCHASE

You can make a one-time "Remove ads" purchase inside the app. After it, the app shows no ads and does not request them from Google AdMob. The purchase is handled entirely by the store you buy it from (Google Play on Android, or the App Store on iPhone): your payment details and purchase history are processed by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies and terms, and are never shared with us. The app only stores a note on your device that ads have been removed, and asks the store to confirm your purchase so it can be restored if you reinstall the app or use another device signed in to the same account.

YOUR CONSENT CHOICES

If you are in a region where consent is required, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent message the first time you open it, and you can choose whether to allow personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings screen.

NOTIFICATIONS

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app asks for permission to show notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device and contain no tracking; the reminder text uses your first name and zodiac sign, which never leave your device. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings screen or in your device's notification settings at any time.

SHARING A READING

When you choose to share a reading, the app hands its text to the app you pick, using your device's standard share feature. We do not receive a copy, and what happens next is controlled by the app you shared with.

WHAT WE DON'T DO

We do not ask for your email address, contacts, photos, location permission or any account login. We do not sell your information, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Fortune Horoscope is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help.

CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when the app gains new features. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.

CONTACT US

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@soluntion.com.
